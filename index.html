For health plans

Protect refill continuity before access is disrupted.

Use this screening view to choose the next question, not to declare compliance, predict savings, or assume every member can use a listed pharmacy.

01

Validate usable access

Confirm addresses, current operations, hours, public eligibility and plan participation. Replace straight-line travel estimates with dated road-network routing.

02

Locate single-access dependencies

Review tracts with one pharmacy in the supplied range, then test realistic alternatives. Treat the flag as a screening signal, not a prediction that a pharmacy will close.

03

Match the response to the barrier

Evaluate transportation, delivery, remote dispensing or lockers alongside local pharmacy support. These services are distinct options, not interchangeable substitutes.

04

Measure what happens next

In a governed member-level analysis, track completed transfers, successful fills, refill gaps and measure-specific proportion of days covered. This public map contains no member data.

Data & interpretation

Keep the analysis versions distinct.

A new display is not an independent rerun. This update imports the attached workbook’s tract records and checks their internal reconciliation.

Mapped here

Supplied October poster scenario

Inventory and results
The workbook describes 495 accessible outlets and 993 populated tracts; 194 are flagged as deserts. The 495-site roster (names, street addresses and geocoded coordinates) is plotted on the map.
Population and geography
The workbook identifies 2020 Census centers-of-population counts totaling 4,237,256, with population-weighted origins. Those origins were not independently verified here. All 993 GEOIDs join the site’s existing 2020 TIGER-based geometry; eight additional shapes have no workbook record.
Social vulnerability
The workbook describes Oregon-ranked CDC/ATSDR SVI 2022, with high SVI ≥0.75. Its ACS demographic counts use 2018–2022 estimates and are distinct from its 2020 resident totals. CDC SVI documentation ↗

494 retained records
The earlier approved v2 screening retained 495 records before excluding Pet Pharmacy NW from the human cohort. The resulting 494 comprise 448 community candidates, 41 unresolved-scope records, three differentiated-service records, one kiosk and one remote-dispensing record.
Not interchangeable with 495
The new poster scenario uses a 495-outlet count. Its reconciliation with the approved veterinary exclusion and separate service categories remains unresolved. The map shows them as the poster cohort’s community pharmacies, not independently verified storefronts.
Pharmacy map markers
The map now plots the 495 poster-cohort community pharmacies (name, street address and coordinates from the poster analysis roster), with the 141 keystone pharmacies highlighted. The older 663-license point layer has been removed from the map.
How to interpret the map

Source-described rule: urban tracts (≥500 people/sq. mile) more than 1 mile, rural tracts more than 10 miles, or more than 15 minutes estimated drive from a pharmacy. Estimated drive time is straight-line distance ×1.3 at 25 mph urban or 40 mph rural; it is not road-network routing.

Missing scores: the workbook has 992 valid SVI scores and one populated tract without a valid score. That tract is labeled SVI unavailable, not low vulnerability. Eight other geometries are not in the workbook.

What was checked: 993 unique GEOIDs, all geometry matches, statewide and tri-county population/category totals, and all 36 county summaries. No new pharmacy eligibility review, address correction, population-origin validation or access computation was performed for this web update.

What cannot be inferred: individual access, payer-network participation, hours, stock, causal effects, adherence improvements, regulatory compliance or cost savings. SVI is an area-level context measure, not an individual diagnosis.

Future data and routing upgrades

Reconcile the 495-outlet scenario against the approved human-access inventory, validate address-level coordinates and population-weighted origins, then rerun access with a dated road network. Preserve separate results for restricted services, specialty/mail order, remote dispensing, kiosks and lockers.
